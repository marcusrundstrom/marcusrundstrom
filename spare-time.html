Hobbies and Interests

Outside of my professional life, I enjoy pursuing various activities that help me maintain a balanced lifestyle and explore different interests.

Reading

I have a deep passion for reading and exploring diverse genres of literature. Whether it's academic papers, fiction, or non-fiction, reading helps me stay informed about current topics and gain new perspectives. I particularly enjoy books on economics, history, and philosophy.

Outdoor Activities

Staying active outdoors is an important part of my life. I enjoy hiking through Sweden's beautiful nature, cycling along scenic routes, and simply enjoying the fresh air. These activities help me maintain physical fitness while providing mental clarity and relaxation away from my research work.

Photography

Photography allows me to capture moments and explore creative perspectives. I enjoy taking photos during my travels and outdoor adventures, focusing on landscapes, architecture, and candid moments. It's a creative outlet that helps me see the world from different angles.

Cooking

Experimenting with different cuisines and recipes is one of my favorite ways to unwind. I enjoy trying new ingredients and cooking techniques, often inspired by dishes I've encountered during travels. Cooking is both a creative process and a practical skill that I continue to develop.

Community Involvement

I believe in giving back to the community through volunteer work and participation in local initiatives. Whether it's contributing to educational programs, supporting local organizations, or engaging in community discussions, I find it important to use my skills and knowledge to make a positive impact beyond my professional work.
